## 🎨 Diagram Details & Structure

### Figure 47: MDRRMO Administrator (Super User) Hierarchy Tree
* **Root Module:** Flood Monitoring & Disaster Response Management System
* **Level 1 (User Role):** MDRRMO Administrator (Command Center Portal)
* **6 Main Branches:**
  1. **Dashboard:** Live River Water Level, Real-Time CCTV Stream, Active Dispatch Summary, Shelter Occupancy Status.
  2. **Water Level Monitoring:** AI Water Detection (C310), Rate of Rise Analysis, Threshold Configuration, Historical Sensor Logs.
  3. **Early Warning Siren & SMS:** Manual Siren Trigger, Automated Cresting Alarm, SMS Broadcast Engine, Barangay Advisory Logs.
  4. **Emergency SOS & Rescue:** Incoming Distress Tickets, Triage Vulnerabilities, Assign Rescue Unit, Live Rescue Telemetry.
  5. **Evacuation & Relief:** Shelter Capacity Tracker, Overflow Reroute Alert, Relief Pack Inventory, DROMIC Report Summary.
  6. **System Settings:** User Account Matrix, Role Access Control, Camera Calibration, Audit Logs & Backup.

---

### Figure 48: Community Resident (Mobile App User) Hierarchy Tree
* **Root Module:** ResQConnect Mobile Application
* **Level 1 (User Role):** Community Resident
* **4 Main Branches:**
  1. **Dashboard:** River Water Level Status, Color-Coded Flood Warning, Live Camera Stream, Barangay Advisories.
  2. **Early Warning Alerts:** Push Alert Notifications, SMS Critical Warnings, Siren Alarm Broadcast, Evacuation Directives.
  3. **Emergency SOS:** One-Tap Distress Beacon, GPS Pinpoint Coordinates, Family Headcount & Needs, Live Rescue Status (ETA).
  4. **Evacuation & Relief:** Nearest Evacuation Shelter, Real-Time Capacity & Rooms, Interactive Route Map, Family Relief QR / Pass.
